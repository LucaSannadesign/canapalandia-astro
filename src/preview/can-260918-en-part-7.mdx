### Does myrcene make you sleepy?

Human clinical evidence is not sufficient to conclude that the presence of myrcene in a hemp product causes sleep or relaxation in a person. Some preclinical studies on isolated molecules describe biological activity, but those results cannot be transferred automatically to a real product, a specific dose or a particular route of administration.

### Are terpenes lost during drying?

The profile can change substantially. A 2024 study on three varieties of *Cannabis sativa* showed that drying method and storage conditions alter the profile of volatile organic compounds and that the volatilome does not remain unchanged over time under the tested conditions.
