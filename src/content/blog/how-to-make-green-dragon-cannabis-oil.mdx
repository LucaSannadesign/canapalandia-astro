<p>Cannabis oil, also referred to as Green Dragon, is a tincture made by extracting cannabinoids such as THC and CBD through alcohol infusion. This method has ancient roots and has been used for centuries as a natural remedy to treat various ailments, but it fell out of use with the advent of prohibition.</p>
<h2>What is Cannabis Decarboxylation?</h2>
<p>Before using cannabis to create oil, it’s essential to carry out a process known as decarboxylation. This chemical process removes a carbon group from THCA, converting it into THC, the active and psychoactive form of the compound.</p>
<p>Decarboxylation happens naturally when cannabis is heated, such as during combustion or vaporization. Heat is the catalyst that transforms non-psychoactive THCA into THC, activating the desired properties of cannabis.</p>
<h2>How to Make Cannabis Oil Tincture</h2>
<p>The cannabis oil tincture is made using a traditional THC extraction method, with alcohol serving as the solvent. Here are the steps:</p>
<ul>
<li>Grind the cannabis flowers using a grinder.</li>
<li>Place the ground cannabis in a glass jar and cover it with high-proof alcohol.</li>
<li>Let the mixture sit for two weeks, shaking it daily to help extract the cannabinoids.</li>
<li>Strain the liquid using a cheesecloth or fine mesh to remove the plant material.</li>
<li>Store the tincture in a dark-colored bottle with a dropper for easy dosing.</li>
<li>Take the tincture sublingually (under the tongue) for rapid absorption.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Benefits of Cannabis Oil Tincture</h2>
<p>Using a tincture allows for precise dosage control and avoids the harmful effects of combustion, making it one of the healthiest methods for consuming THC. Some of the benefits include:</p>
<ul>
<li>**Precise Dosing**: Tinctures allow for controlled and accurate dosing, which is especially important for medical cannabis users.</li>
<li>**Discreet Use**: Cannabis tinctures are odorless and easy to consume, making them a discreet option for users who want to avoid smoking or vaping.</li>
<li>**Fast Absorption**: Taking the tincture sublingually ensures that cannabinoids are absorbed quickly into the bloodstream, leading to faster effects compared to edibles.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Final Tip</h2>
<p>To ensure the best quality of your tincture, follow detailed video tutorials or instructions to avoid mistakes during the extraction process.</p>
