<h2>Introduction</h2>
<p>The <strong>history</strong> of <strong>therapeutic cannabis</strong> is as ancient as humanity itself. For millennia, this plant has accompanied humankind as a natural remedy, enduring periods of acceptance and prohibition. Today, while many parts of the world are finally recognizing its medical value, Italy continues to struggle with ambiguous laws and partial information.</p>

<h2>Origins and Ancient Uses</h2>
<p><strong>Cannabis</strong> has a <strong>history</strong> rooted in antiquity. In China, as early as 5,000 years ago, Emperor Shen Nung used it to treat rheumatism and intestinal problems. In India, it was considered a sacred plant, capable of relieving physical and mental suffering.</p>

<h2>Current Legislation</h2>
<p>In Italy, <strong>therapeutic cannabis</strong> has been legal since 2007, but availability is often limited. National production, entrusted to the Military Chemical-Pharmaceutical Plant in Florence, does not meet demand, forcing imports from countries like the Netherlands and Canada. A prescription is required, and the preparation is carried out by authorized galenic pharmacies (<a href="https://www.farmagalenica.it">Farmagalenica</a>).</p>

<h2>Modern Cultivation Techniques</h2>
<p>For those who want to legally cultivate <strong>therapeutic cannabis</strong>, the challenges are many. It is essential to select certified seeds and ensure a controlled cultivation environment to keep THC within legal limits (0.2%). Among the most used techniques are:</p>
<ul>
    <li>Indoor cultivation with climate control</li>
    <li>Use of organic substrates to avoid contamination</li>
    <li>Constant monitoring of cannabinoid levels</li>
</ul>
<p>The cultivated varieties must be certified and comply with GMP standards (<a href="https://www.farmaciaassarotti.it">Farmacia Assarotti</a>).</p>

<h2>Choosing the Right Seeds</h2>
<p>To ensure a legal and quality production, it is essential to choose certified varieties of <strong>therapeutic cannabis</strong>. Among the most used are:</p>
<ul>
    <li>Bedrocan: high THC concentration</li>
    <li>Bedrolite: higher CBD concentration</li>
    <li>FM2: Italian variety produced by the Military Chemical-Pharmaceutical Plant in Florence</li>
</ul>

<h2>Scientific Evidence</h2>
<p>Recent studies indicate that specific combinations of CBD and THC can be effective in treating pain and anxiety. In particular, CBD:THC ratios between 3:1 and 6:1 have shown promising results (<a href="https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2023034568A1/en">Google Patents</a>).</p>

<h2>Conclusions</h2>
<p>The <strong>history of therapeutic cannabis</strong> is a journey of ups and downs, between traditional uses and modern prohibitions. Today, despite scientific evidence and growing international acceptance, Italy continues to lag behind, hampered by unclear laws and entrenched prejudice.</p>

<h2>Editorial Opinion</h2>
<p>Continuing to treat cannabis as a public enemy is like trying to stop the wind with your hands. The scientific data is clear: this plant has significant therapeutic potential. Perhaps it is time to move beyond moralism and start regulating intelligently, looking to the future instead of clinging to outdated prejudices.</p>
