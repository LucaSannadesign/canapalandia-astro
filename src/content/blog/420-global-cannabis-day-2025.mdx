<p>April 20th isn’t just a date on the calendar — it’s <strong>420</strong>, the <strong>International Cannabis Day</strong>. A celebration born on American campuses that has grown into a global symbol of <strong>cultural resistance</strong>, <strong>independent information</strong>, and <strong>anti-prohibition activism</strong>.</p>

<p>But in Italy, while the world moves forward, the 420 is celebrated while holding our breath. The new <strong>2025 Security Decree</strong> tightens the grip on <strong>CBD and medical cannabis</strong>, and the plants of freedom risk withering under mandatory prescriptions and absurd controls.</p>

<h2>🕰️ The Origins of 420: From Secret Code to Global Symbol</h2>

<p>The story of 420 began in <strong>California</strong> in the early 1970s. A group of high school students in San Rafael — known as the “<strong>Waldos</strong>” — used to meet at <strong>4:20 PM</strong> every day to search for a mysterious abandoned marijuana plant near a Coast Guard base.</p>

<p>420 became their private code, a discreet way to talk about cannabis. But that code soon spread beyond their school, thanks to the underground scene and the <strong>Grateful Dead</strong> community.</p>

<p>By the 1990s, <em>High Times</em> magazine embraced it as a symbol, and ever since then, <strong>“420” has become a universal reference</strong> for those who believe in legalization, hemp culture, and the right to choose.</p>

<h2>🌿 Why is 420 Celebrated?</h2>

<p>Today, 420 is marked around the world with <strong>events, gatherings, protests, and educational content</strong>. It’s a day to unite, to inform, and to reflect.</p>

<p>It’s not just a celebration — it’s a <strong>cultural statement</strong>, a stand against prohibition and in favor of freedom: freedom to heal, to choose, to speak out.</p>

<p>This year, <strong>420 coincides with Easter</strong>. And like in many moments of cultural expression, we chose to use <strong>powerful symbols</strong> to speak from the heart.</p>

<h2>🇮🇹 What’s Going On in Italy?</h2>

<p>In Italy, things are more absurd than ever. On one hand, medical cannabis is <strong>legal but heavily restricted</strong>. On the other, light cannabis is treated like a public threat. And meanwhile, patients struggle just to access a drop of CBD oil.</p>

<p>But <strong>420 lives on</strong>, even here. In the voices of lawyers challenging the new decree, in the testimonies of patients, and in the work of those like Canapalandia, who keep spreading <strong>counter-information</strong>.</p>
