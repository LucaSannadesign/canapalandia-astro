<p><em>April 25 is not only <strong>Liberation Day</strong> from fascism—this year it also coincides with national mourning for Pope Francis and the enforcement of the <strong>Security Decree 2025</strong>, which criminalizes <strong>light cannabis</strong>.</em></p>

<h2>April 25 and the Value of Freedom</h2>
<p>On April 25, 1945, Italy threw off an authoritarian regime and embraced democracy. Today, those same ideals of freedom and self-determination are threatened by laws that treat a non-psychoactive plant as an illicit drug. Honoring the historic <strong>Resistance</strong> means defending the right to informed, responsible choice.</p>

<h2>National Mourning and Temporary Bans</h2>
<p>Following the passing of Pope Francis on April 21, 2025, the Council of Ministers declared five days of national mourning, during which:</p>
<ul>
  <li>flags fly at half-mast;</li>
  <li>public events and entertainment are suspended;</li>
  <li>unauthorized gatherings are prohibited;</li>
  <li>some official ceremonies are postponed or simplified.</li>
</ul>
<p>While these measures pay tribute to the late pontiff, they overlay the traditional April 25 commemorations with an atmosphere of solemn restraint.</p>

<h2>What the Security Decree 2025 Provides</h2>
<p>Passed under emergency procedure, the <strong>Security Decree 2025</strong> reclassifies <strong>light cannabis</strong> (THC &lt; 0.6 %) as a controlled substance. Key provisions include:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Ban</strong> on cultivation, production, sale and possession of light cannabis flowers;</li>
  <li><strong>Fines</strong> up to €10,000 and potential criminal charges for operators and users;</li>
  <li><strong>Automatic seizure</strong> of all stock without compensation;</li>
  <li><strong>Exceptions</strong> only for medical use (by specialist prescription) and select industrial applications (fiber, seed).</li>
</ul>
<p>According to Coldiretti, the light cannabis sector was worth over €500 million and employed 25,000 people. With the decree in force, many businesses have already halted operations, imperiling rural economies.</p>

<h2>Historic Resistance vs. “Normative Liberation”</h2>
<p>Just as partisans fought to free Italy from oppression, today’s light cannabis growers embody a new <strong>“Green Resistance”</strong>. Ironically, these “modern partisans” are waging a battle against laws that ignore science and sustainable opportunities.</p>

<h2>Practical Consequences for the Sector</h2>
<p>The decree’s immediate impact:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Harvests halted</strong> in full season, fields abandoned;</li>
  <li><strong>Closure</strong> of over 300 grow-shops nationwide;</li>
  <li><strong>Loss</strong> of around 10,000 jobs within months;</li>
  <li><strong>Collapse</strong> of a local-development model that revived depopulated villages.</li>
</ul>
<p>Industry groups speak of environmental, social and economic damage as hundreds of hectares lie unused and families lose their livelihoods.</p>

<h2>Strategies for the “New Fight”</h2>
<p>To counter the <strong>Security Decree 2025</strong> and reclaim fair regulation, action is needed on multiple fronts:</p>
<h3>1. Legal and Constitutional Appeals</h3>
<p>Support lawsuits grounded in toxicological evidence and the principle of proportionality enshrined in the Constitution and EU law.</p>
<h3>2. Civic Mobilization</h3>
<p>Join webinars, forums and online assemblies organized by groups such as Italia Cannabis and Union Canapa to build an informed public network.</p>
<h3>3. Diversification of Production</h3>
<p>Invest in alternative hemp uses—bio-construction, textiles, natural cosmetics—to reduce dependence on the CBD market and strengthen rural resilience.</p>
