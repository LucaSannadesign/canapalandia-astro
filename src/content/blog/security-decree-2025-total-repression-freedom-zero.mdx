---<p class="p1"><span class="s1"><b>April 11, 2025</b></span> – President Sergio Mattarella has signed the <span class="s1"><b>Security Decree</b></span>, approved by the Council of Ministers on April 4. Despite modifications from the initial draft, this act introduces measures that raise serious concerns regarding <span class="s1"><b>civil liberties</b></span>, <span class="s1"><b>fundamental rights</b></span>, and the very <span class="s1"><b>legality of the Republic</b></span>.</p>
<h3><b>📜 </b></h3>
<h3><b>Key Provisions of the Security Decree</b></h3>
<p class="p1">Blocking a road is no longer a mere administrative offense but a criminal act. <span class="s1"><b>Passive resistance</b></span> is criminalized. <span class="s1"><b>Occupying properties</b></span>, even out of housing necessity, can lead to up to seven years in prison. The message is clear: <span class="s1"><b>silence and compliance</b></span>.</p>
<p class="p1">A new form of <span class="s1"><b>detention for mothers</b></span> is introduced, limiting humanitarian benefits. Police officers will be financially covered by the state even in cases of criminal proceedings. And, to top it off, <span class="s1"><b>legal hemp</b></span> is punished for being… legal.</p>
<h3><b>🌿 </b></h3>
<h3><b>The Cannabis Case: Unjustified Repression</b></h3>
<p class="p1">With the decree, <span class="s1"><b>light cannabis</b></span> is officially equated to a narcotic substance. <span class="s1"><b>Flowers, oils, resins, extracts</b></span> are banned, even if they contain less than 0.2% THC. The only exception concerns seeds, which, however, cannot be cultivated to obtain flowers. It’s the triumph of <span class="s1"><b>repressive confusion</b></span>.</p>
<p class="p1">The damage is enormous: a completely legal sector, based on <span class="s1"><b>European regulations</b></span> and recognized by rulings of the <span class="s1"><b>Court of Cassation</b></span> and the <span class="s1"><b>EU Court of Justice</b></span>, is targeted.</p>
<h3><b>💥 </b></h3>
<h3><b>Devastating Impacts on the Sector</b></h3>
<p class="p1">The legal hemp market in Italy was worth over <span class="s1"><b>2 billion euros</b></span>. Approximately <span class="s1"><b>22,000 jobs</b></span> are now at risk. This includes farmers, processors, researchers, wellness professionals, retailers, e-commerce startups, artisans, and merchants. All <span class="s1"><b>criminalized overnight</b></span>.</p>
<p class="p1">Many risk criminal proceedings for having stored products now banned. <span class="s1"><b>No transition phase</b></span> is provided. Those working in hemp are treated like drug dealers. It’s the end of a sector and the end of logic.</p>
<h3><b>⚖️ </b></h3>
<h3><b>Conflict with the European Union</b></h3>
<p class="p1">The <span class="s1"><b>Court of Justice of the European Union</b></span> has repeatedly stated that <span class="s1"><b>CBD is not a narcotic</b></span>. Preventing the free movement of these products among member states is <span class="s1"><b>illegal</b></span>. The Italian decree <span class="s1"><b>violates EU law</b></span> and exposes the country to sanctions and lawsuits.</p>
<p class="p1">Italy isolates itself while <span class="s1"><b>Germany</b></span>, <span class="s1"><b>Switzerland</b></span>, and the <span class="s1"><b>Czech Republic</b></span> regulate cannabis in a modern way. We return to the past, with the bogeyman of the joint as a threat to national security.</p>
<h3><b>🔒 </b></h3>
<h3><b>The Strategy of Total Control</b></h3>
<p class="p1">The decree has a clear objective: to <span class="s1"><b>suppress dissent, critical thinking, and economic independence</b></span>. Those who cultivate hemp, protest in the streets, occupy abandoned buildings, or demand more rights must remain silent and obedient.</p>
<p class="p1">It’s an <span class="s1"><b>ideological attack</b></span>, using security as a Trojan horse to gag citizens’ autonomy. The most vulnerable, the bravest, the most innovative will suffer.</p>
<h3><b>🧨 </b></h3>
<h3><b>Collateral Effects: Citizens in the Crosshairs</b></h3>
<p class="p1">Those possessing legal hemp-based products risk sanctions. Peaceful protesters risk years in prison. Those trying to change something in this country are punished. The message is clear: <span class="s1"><b>freedom is a crime</b></span>.</p>
<p class="p1">But in such a climate, security will not prevail; instead, fear, suspicion, and silence will. The price? A <span class="s1"><b>poorer, more ignorant, more unjust</b></span> country.</p>
<h3><b>✊ </b></h3>
<h3><b>Reactions and Mobilizations</b></h3>
<p class="p4"><span class="s3">Industry associations – from </span><b>Canapa Sativa Italia</b><span class="s3"> to </span><b>Confagricoltura</b><span class="s3">, from </span><b>Federcanapa</b><span class="s3"> to </span><b>Coldiretti</b><span class="s3"> – have launched a national mobilization. </span><b>Appeals to the TAR</b><span class="s3">, </span><b>complaints to Brussels</b><span class="s3">, </span><b>popular petitions</b><span class="s3">, and </span><b>public demonstrations</b><span class="s3"> are being prepared throughout Italy.</span></p>
<p class="p1">Jurists, doctors, economists, and opposition parliamentarians have also denounced the unconstitutionality of the decree. But a strong grassroots response is needed: <span class="s1"><b>we cannot stand by</b></span>.</p>
<h3><b>🧭 </b></h3>
<h3><b>Conclusion: A Decree Against Intelligence</b></h3>
<p class="p1">This decree does not serve to guarantee security. It serves to <span class="s1"><b>cover up the social, economic, and political disaster</b></span> of an incapable government. It serves to <span class="s1"><b>distract public opinion</b></span>. It serves to <span class="s1"><b>maintain power by targeting the weakest</b></span>.</p>
<p class="p1">We cannot accept this in silence. Defending hemp today means defending the right to live in a <span class="s1"><b>free, sustainable, intelligent</b></span> country. And we will not let this right be taken away.</p>
