<h2><strong>🔬 CBD and THC: What Are They and How Do They Work?</strong></h2>
<h3><strong>📌 What is CBD (Cannabidiol)?</strong></h3>
<p><strong>CBD (cannabidiol)</strong> is a phytocannabinoid found in the cannabis plant. It is known for its <strong>relaxing, anti-inflammatory, and neuroprotective</strong> effects. Unlike THC, <strong>CBD is non-psychoactive</strong>, meaning it does not alter perception or cognitive function.</p>
<p>📌 <strong>Key Characteristics of CBD:</strong><br />✅ <strong>Non-psychoactive</strong> (does not alter perception)<br />✅ <strong>Interacts with the endocannabinoid system without directly binding to CB1 receptors</strong><br />✅ <strong>Can reduce anxiety, pain, and inflammation</strong><br />✅ <strong>Studied for its effectiveness in treating epilepsy, sleep disorders, stress, and inflammation</strong><br />✅ <strong>Legal in many countries with THC content restrictions</strong></p>
<hr />
<h3><strong>📌 What is THC (Tetrahydrocannabinol)?</strong></h3>
<p><strong>THC (tetrahydrocannabinol)</strong> is the primary psychoactive compound in cannabis. It is responsible for the <strong>euphoric and psychotropic effects</strong> that make cannabis popular for recreational use.</p>
<p>📌 <strong>Key Characteristics of THC:</strong><br />✅ <strong>Directly binds to CB1 receptors in the brain, causing psychoactive effects</strong><br />✅ <strong>Produces feelings of euphoria, relaxation, altered perception, and increased appetite</strong><br />✅ <strong>Used to treat chronic pain, nausea, and muscle spasms</strong><br />✅ <strong>Can cause side effects such as anxiety, paranoia, and increased heart rate</strong><br />✅ <strong>Illegal in many countries but regulated for medical use in some states</strong></p>
<hr />
<h2><strong>⚙️ How Do They Affect the Body: The Endocannabinoid System</strong></h2>
<p>The <strong>Endocannabinoid System (ECS)</strong> is a regulatory network in our body responsible for controlling essential functions such as:</p>
<ul>
<li>Sleep</li>
<li>Pain management</li>
<li>Mood</li>
<li>Inflammation</li>
<li>Memory</li>
</ul>
<p>The main <strong>cannabinoid receptors</strong> in the body are:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>CB1</strong> → Found primarily in the brain and central nervous system, responsible for THC’s psychoactive effects.</li>
<li><strong>CB2</strong> → Located in the immune system, involved in anti-inflammatory and neuroprotective processes.</li>
</ul>
<p>📌 <strong>How do CBD and THC interact with these receptors?</strong><br />✔ <strong>THC binds directly to CB1 receptors</strong>, causing psychoactive effects.<br />✔ <strong>CBD modulates receptors without directly activating them</strong>, counteracting some of THC’s effects and promoting relaxation and wellness.</p>
