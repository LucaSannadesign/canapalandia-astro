<h3 class="wp-block-heading">&lt;p&gt;Marco Cappato, the well-known political activist and treasurer of the Luca Coscioni Association, has announced a revolutionary initiative: the distribution of cannabis seeds through his website. With a simple donation of just €3, anyone can receive a cannabis seed directly at home. This initiative is part of a broader civil disobedience campaign aimed at pushing the debate on cannabis legalization in Italy.&lt;/p&gt;</h3>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">&lt;h2&gt;A Symbolic Gesture to Break the Stalemate&lt;/h2&gt;</h3>

<p>&lt;p&gt;In a video posted online, Cappato explained that this initiative is a way to protest the long-standing legislative deadlock in Italy regarding the legalization of cannabis cultivation and use. In Italy, the cultivation of cannabis is still heavily penalized by current laws, despite growing demand and shifting global attitudes.&lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt;Currently, the illegal cannabis market in Italy is estimated to serve around 4 million consumers who, being forced to seek cannabis on the black market, enrich criminal organizations and fuel the illicit drug trade. This makes legislative action even more urgent to regulate the situation and remove control of cannabis from criminal networks.&lt;/p&gt;</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">&lt;h2&gt;The Luca Coscioni Association&#8217;s Battle&lt;/h2&gt;</h3>

<p>&lt;p&gt;Marco Cappato&#8217;s action is not isolated; it fits within the broader efforts of the Luca Coscioni Association, which has long been at the forefront of civil rights advocacy and promoting freedom of scientific research. The association, in collaboration with jurists, experts, and anti-prohibition activists, is preparing to file a lawsuit against the Italian government for serious failings in regulating cannabis and its cultivation.&lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt;&#8221;At the same time, we will take legal action against the government for serious non-compliance,&#8221; Cappato declared, emphasizing that Italy&#8217;s delay in addressing cannabis legislation is damaging both the legal system and society.&lt;/p&gt;</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">&lt;h2&gt;An Expanding Black Market&lt;/h2&gt;</h3>

<p>&lt;p&gt;In Italy, the ban on cannabis cultivation has created a vast black market that not only fuels organized crime but also endangers consumers&#8217; health. Buying cannabis on the black market often means having no guarantee of product quality or safety. Harmful substances can be added to increase weight or potency, putting consumers at risk.&lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt;According to Cappato and the Luca Coscioni Association, legalizing cannabis cultivation and consumption would break this vicious cycle, allowing consumers to grow their own cannabis safely and under regulated conditions.&lt;/p&gt;</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">&lt;h2&gt;How the Initiative Works&lt;/h2&gt;</h3>

<p>&lt;p&gt;Participating in Marco Cappato&#8217;s initiative is simple. By visiting his website, users can make a €3 donation to receive a cannabis seed at home. This symbolic gesture serves as a protest against the current legal restrictions and aims to spur public debate on cannabis legalization in Italy.&lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt;&#8221;With this action, I want to draw attention to the need to change unjust laws that penalize consumers and criminalize a plant that, in many other countries, has already been regulated or legalized,&#8221; Cappato explained. Through this campaign, he hopes to spark a movement that will lead Italy down the path of legalization, following the example of nations like Canada and several U.S. states.&lt;/p&gt;</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">&lt;h2&gt;Final Reflections&lt;/h2&gt;</h3>

<p>&lt;p&gt;Marco Cappato&#8217;s initiative is not just a provocation but a genuine call for change. With his action, he urges the Italian government to reconsider current legislation and finally open the door to cannabis legalization in Italy. Cappato&#8217;s fight is also a fight for freedom of choice and freedom of scientific research, values that have always been central to the Luca Coscioni Association&#8217;s mission.&lt;/p&gt; &lt;p&gt;If you want to learn more about the initiative or contribute to the seed distribution campaign, you can visit Marco Cappato&#8217;s website and join this cause for a more just and free society.&lt;/p&gt;Se vuoi saperne di più sull’iniziativa o contribuire alla campagna di distribuzione dei semi, puoi visitare il sito web di Marco Cappato e unirti a questa causa per una società più giusta e libera.</p>
